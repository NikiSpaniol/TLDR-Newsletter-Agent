Researchers analyzed 137,000 posts from the r/ChatGPT subreddit spanning December 2022 to December 2025 using sparse autoencoders to identify topic trends and their evolution. Key findings show that user engagement shifted from philosophical curiosity about AI to practical incorporation into daily workflows, with a dramatic inflection point after GPT-4o's May 2024 release when posts about emotional engagement (therapy and companionship) surged to roughly 10-15 percent of all subreddit content. HOW WE CATCH MESSAGING CAMPAIGN DRIFT BEFORE IT SPREADS (8 MINUTE READ)

hook

Infobip, a messaging platform, built a multi-stage system to detect when approved messaging campaigns drift from their original intent at scale—processing approximately 300 million SMS and RCS messages daily.

Infobip, a messaging platform, built a multi-stage system to detect when approved messaging campaigns drift from their original intent at scale—processing approximately 300 million SMS and RCS messages daily. The system groups messages by semantic similarity using embedding vectors rather than keyword matching, classifying incoming messages against known approved clusters in real time or routing novel content to a discovery path for later analysis. When new message clusters form, an LLM evaluates them against the original campaign metadata and flags potential violations like brand impersonation or phishing attempts, with human reviewers making final decisions on severity and action. The architecture uses clustering as a leverage point to amortize the cost of LLM inference across templated, repetitive campaign messages instead of judging each message individually. Key design choices include keeping clusters fixed to surface semantic shifts as new groups and representing each cluster with select examples rather than full membership. The company acknowledges tradeoffs including overclustering from embedding models that treat benign surface variations differently, discovery latency in the deferred path, and cluster proliferation from legitimate message variation.

Strategic hookUnderstanding how customers' messaging campaigns drift over time provides critical input for designing content governance policies and identifying when a sender's actual behavior has diverged from their stated business intent and approved use case.

HOW WRONG IS YOUR MARKETING MIX MODEL (MMM)? (21 MINUTE READ)

hook

Marketing Mix Modeling is difficult because marketing channels typically move together in correlated patterns that prevent statistical models from isolating each channel's true impact.

Marketing Mix Modeling is difficult because marketing channels typically move together in correlated patterns that prevent statistical models from isolating each channel's true impact. Google's 2017 research identified three specific problems: multi-collinearity creates high variance in estimates, selection bias (spend following demand) creates persistent upward bias in channel coefficients, and insufficient spend variation makes adstock and saturation parameters unidentifiable. This article presents a simulation study testing whether a budget phasing algorithm—which intentionally varies channel spend through dark months, peak months, and weekly nudges—can address all three issues simultaneously. The "Combined" strategy reduced variance from 0.23 to 0.07, bias from 28.8% to 15.3%, and made saturation and adstock ranges much tighter, though at a cost of 3.42% in foregone revenue and occasional platform learning disruptions.

Strategic hookUnderstanding how to design spending patterns that reveal true channel performance is critical for building accurate business cases for marketing budget allocation across channels.

DESKTOP HABITS HIDE MOBILE FRICTION (2 MINUTE READ)

hook

Most ecommerce stores suffer from poor mobile experiences not because of neglect but because decision-makers only view their sites on desktop, missing critical friction points that affect cold social traffic arriving via Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook in-app browsers.

Most ecommerce stores suffer from poor mobile experiences not because of neglect but because decision-makers only view their sites on desktop, missing critical friction points that affect cold social traffic arriving via Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook in-app browsers. The article identifies four major mobile friction sources: excessive pop-ups (cookie consent, age checks, email signups) that should be timed or delayed rather than immediate; design elements like sticky headers and add-to-cart bars that consume screen real estate when stacked together on actual phones; tiny fonts and light gray serif text optimized for AI agents rather than human shoppers; and interaction limitations like disabled pinch-to-zoom on product images and size charts that work for mouse cursors but frustrate finger-driven navigation. The core insight is that returning customers tolerate friction, but cold social visitors—arriving unaware with no saved login or cart history—will simply revert to the app they came from if the mobile experience isn't frictionless. The article recommends testing sites in incognito mode on real phones, A/B testing pop-up timing, measuring actual screen space after all elements load, prioritizing human readability over AI optimization, and ensuring touch-friendly interactions like functional image zooming.

Strategic hookUnderstanding how different customer acquisition channels (organic social versus returning visitors) experience fundamentally different friction points should directly inform product roadmap prioritization and mobile UX testing strategy, as cold social traffic has zero tolerance for barriers that loyal customers will tolerate.

HOW WE INCREASED ORDERS BY 9% WITHOUT A DISCOUNT CODE (2 MINUTE READ)

hook

A South American gift company conducting funnel analysis discovered high dropout rates at the payment stage of checkout.

A South American gift company conducting funnel analysis discovered high dropout rates at the payment stage of checkout. Customer research revealed that visitors felt the final price lacked value justification and many wanted a discount code. Rather than discounting, the company added two lines to the checkout order table naming free services already included with purchase—a white-glove wrapping service and a satisfaction guarantee—both marked as FREE. This simple reframing of the value proposition, which cost the client nothing extra, increased orders by 9%. The insight applies the framing effect principle that prices are judged relative to perceived value, not in isolation, and that reassurance at the moment of payment is particularly impactful.

Strategic hookThis demonstrates how customer research into purchase objections can uncover that price resistance often signals unclear value communication rather than actual cost concerns, enabling companies to reposition existing benefits to improve conversion without margin erosion.

BEFORE YOU REBRAND: HOW A BRAND AUDIT REVEALS WHAT ACTUALLY NEEDS TO CHANGE (5 MINUTE READ)

hook

Before rushing to rebrand, companies should conduct a structured brand audit to diagnose whether the real problem is positioning, messaging, execution inconsistency, or identity—since "we need a rebrand" typically describes a symptom, not a cause.

Before rushing to rebrand, companies should conduct a structured brand audit to diagnose whether the real problem is positioning, messaging, execution inconsistency, or identity—since "we need a rebrand" typically describes a symptom, not a cause. A brand audit examines five layers: internal intent, public expression, market perception, competitive context, and experience-versus-promise alignment, then classifies findings into four categories—positioning problems, messaging problems, execution problems, or genuine identity problems—each requiring different solutions. The PepsiCo case study exemplifies this approach: what appeared to be a visual modernization need was actually a perception problem where data science felt intimidating to internal stakeholders; repositioning the story around what data enabled (clearer foresight, better decisions) defined the identity system's actual job. A full rebrand becomes defensible only when evidence shows the identity or name actively blocks business direction, and even then, companies should identify what assets deserve preservation rather than erasing all recognition. AI search has heightened the importance of consistency across sources, since search engines and AI assistants infer company identity from websites, profiles, reviews, and listings; contradictory descriptions create ambiguity that can harm interpretability.

Strategic hookA brand audit directly supports customer strategy by identifying gaps between internal intent and actual customer perception through interviews and research, revealing whether the business's credible differentiation matches what the priority audience understands and values.

IS THIS A JOB FOR A HUMAN OR AI? (5 MINUTE READ)

hook

Richard Jolly, a clinical associate professor at Kellogg School of Management, argues that companies should adopt a strategic framework—the HUMAN framework—to decide which tasks should go to AI and which should remain with humans.

Richard Jolly, a clinical associate professor at Kellogg School of Management, argues that companies should adopt a strategic framework—the HUMAN framework—to decide which tasks should go to AI and which should remain with humans. The framework identifies five categories of relationship-based work that AI cannot effectively replace: Humanizing (genuine empathy and understanding), Unscripted (spontaneous judgment and improvisation), Moral (accountability for consequential decisions), Adaptive (challenging assumptions and disagreement), and Narrative (persuasive storytelling that changes minds). Jolly emphasizes that humans uniquely establish authentic relationships, can be held accountable, embrace productive disagreement, and craft compelling narratives—capabilities critical for leadership and client-facing roles. By delegating routine tasks to AI, companies can free employees to focus on higher-value relationship work, which research shows improves employee engagement and retention, particularly among Gen Z workers seeking meaningful work rather than drudgery.

Strategic hookCompanies developing customer strategy should use the HUMAN framework to identify which customer-facing activities require human relationship-building versus which can be automated, ensuring that client interaction touchpoints retain authentic human engagement that competitors using pure AI automation cannot replicate.
