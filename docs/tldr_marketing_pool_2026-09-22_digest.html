DO CHATGPT & GOOGLE AI GET YOUR BUSINESS DETAILS RIGHT? (7 MINUTE READ)

hook

A study of 600 small businesses found that AI engines give inaccurate information about local businesses roughly one in four times.

A study of 600 small businesses found that AI engines give inaccurate information about local businesses roughly one in four times. Google AI Overviews contained factually wrong details in 27% of answers and ChatGPT in 20%, with errors including incorrect hours, non-existent services, and insurance plans the business never claimed to accept. When a business website is vague or silent, Google invents specifics and presents them as fact 80% of the time, while ChatGPT does so only 10% of the time because it usually discloses its sources. The problem stems partly from businesses themselves—46 of 59 businesses without published hours had their Google Business Profile set to "Open 24 hours," which the engines then accurately report. Accuracy improves dramatically when businesses publish information clearly: to 84% for Google and 89% for ChatGPT, a 16-21 point lift. The article recommends five fixes, most actionable by the business owner within an hour, including correcting Google Business Profile settings, clarifying what "24/7 availability" actually means, and ensuring hours and other information match across all published locations.

Strategic hookUnderstanding how AI systems present business information to prospective customers is critical for developing a customer acquisition strategy, as it directly shapes what information reaches the customer at the critical decision moment before they contact the business.

AI OVERVIEWS CUT CTR BY 23.1% IN FRANCE (5 MINUTE READ)

Google's AI Overviews launched in France on July 22, 2026, and within nine days, domains most exposed to the feature lost 23.1% of their click-through rate, according to analysis of 963 major French domains tracked before and after the rollout.

Google's AI Overviews launched in France on July 22, 2026, and within nine days, domains most exposed to the feature lost 23.1% of their click-through rate, according to analysis of 963 major French domains tracked before and after the rollout. The impact follows a linear relationship: each additional percentage point of queries triggering an AI Overview costs roughly one percentage point of CTR, meaning a site with 15% AI Overview exposure should expect around 15% CTR loss. Impressions remained stable or increased, indicating the feature doesn't remove sites from search results but makes them redundant by answering queries directly in the SERP box. The health sector was hardest hit with a 22.2% AI Overview rate and 20.4% CTR decline, as these informational queries with short answers are most vulnerable to the feature. The situation is expected to worsen over time, mirroring global trends where the impact on position-one CTR worsened from -34.5% in spring 2025 to -58% by year-end 2025 as AI Overviews expanded and users adapted their behavior.

EU TO RESTRICT SOCIAL MEDIA FOR KIDS UNDER 15 (2 MINUTE READ)

The European Union announced the proposed EU KIDS Act on Thursday, which will restrict social media access for children across all 27 member states.

The European Union announced the proposed EU KIDS Act on Thursday, which will restrict social media access for children across all 27 member states. Children under 15 will be banned from creating their own accounts on platforms like TikTok, Instagram, and Snapchat, though those aged 13-14 can use parent-controlled "mini" accounts limited to one hour daily, while children under 13 cannot access social media at all. The law extends beyond social media to cover video-sharing platforms, online gaming, AI chatbots, and app stores, requiring companies with 45 million or more monthly active EU users to demonstrate age-appropriate and safe design by eliminating addictive features like infinite scrolling. Non-compliant companies face fines up to six percent of worldwide annual revenue, and age verification via an EU app or third-party platforms will be mandatory. The measure builds on precedent-setting bans in Australia and similar moves in Canada, the UK, Brazil, and New Zealand, positioning Europe's comprehensive approach within a growing global trend of restricting children's social media access.

An Interactive Advertising Bureau study of 2,200 consumers across five countries found that while 80% are comfortable with AI shopping recommendations, preferences vary significantly by generation and source. Specifically, 65% of Gen Z and millennials prefer when AI cites creator or influencer content, compared to just 34% of boomers, with Gen X split at 53%. Strategic hookBrands need to understand that generational preferences for creator content in AI recommendations do not translate directly to purchase intent, requiring customer segmentation strategies that account for age demographics and journey stage when allocating influencer marketing budgets.

5 LEVERS DRIVE CAC, SAYS AD PRO (3 MINUTE READ)

hook

Hal Smith, founder of H Street Digital, identifies five performance levers that drive customer acquisition cost for direct-to-consumer brands advertising on Meta and Google: the offer, creative quality, account structure, landing page conversion rates, and signal engineering.

Hal Smith, founder of H Street Digital, identifies five performance levers that drive customer acquisition cost for direct-to-consumer brands advertising on Meta and Google: the offer, creative quality, account structure, landing page conversion rates, and signal engineering. Smith emphasizes that effective ads must stop scrolling through novelty and curiosity—often via a hook in the first three seconds of video or headline of static ads—rather than relying on polished, forgettable creative. He stresses the importance of targeting the right customer personas using language those prospects actually use and ensuring ad talent reflects the target audience. A critical optimization mistake Smith cites is brands optimizing for highest volume at lowest price rather than new customer acquisition; instead, brands should set up custom purchase events for new customers as their main optimization goal to train algorithms correctly. Smith recommends a "matrix approach" to creative testing that covers multiple customer personas, angles, and ad formats (static images, GIFs, user-generated videos, etc.) at sufficient scale and budget to generate meaningful conversion data—he notes that a $500 daily budget with a $500 CAC target teaches nothing, while a $10,000 daily budget with a $20 target CAC enables rapid learning.

Strategic hookUnderstanding the five levers that impact CAC—offer, creative, account structure, landing page, and signal engineering—should inform a customer strategy team's competitive analysis of how rivals are optimizing acquisition economics and their own business case for marketing investment levels.

HOW TO READ A DESIGN BRIEF: TRUEROLL GOLF BRAND IDENTITY CASE STUDY (5 MINUTE READ)

Boris Sage of DesWave recounts designing the brand identity for TrueRoll Golf Club, a golf travel platform seeking to book trips and coordinate groups.

Boris Sage of DesWave recounts designing the brand identity for TrueRoll Golf Club, a golf travel platform seeking to book trips and coordinate groups. The pivotal insight came not from the formal brief form but from an offhand comment: TrueRoll's first audience was golf courses and clubs, not individual golfers, meaning it needed to look established and prestigious rather than aspirational and modern.
