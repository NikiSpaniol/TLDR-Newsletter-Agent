THE USEFULNESS OF ATTRIBUTION FOR AI SEARCH (6 MINUTE READ)

hook

Traditional attribution models, designed around cookies and clicks, have become increasingly unreliable as AI search tools like Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT provide answers directly without requiring website visits, while privacy regulations and multi-device usage further fragment customer journey visibility.

Traditional attribution models, designed around cookies and clicks, have become increasingly unreliable as AI search tools like Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT provide answers directly without requiring website visits, while privacy regulations and multi-device usage further fragment customer journey visibility. George Bonaci, VP Growth at Ramp (which processes over $100B in annualized payments), argues that attribution has become a "crutch replacing critical thinking" and that the shift from "search → click → convert" to "prompt → synthesize → direct visit" makes classic attribution models fundamentally inadequate, with AI potentially underattributed by 10x. Rather than abandoning measurement entirely, the article proposes two alternatives: triangulation (combining multiple metrics like Share of Voice, self-reported discovery, and conversions to build confidence) and incrementality testing (using randomized holdouts, geo experiments, or quasi-experiments to measure what actually works). A BCG survey found that 46% of senior measurement professionals use an integrated approach combining multi-touch attribution, incrementality, and marketing mix modeling, and those leaders achieve up to 70% stronger revenue growth. The core insight is that as measurable work becomes commoditized and automatable, competitive advantage shifts to unmeasurable, creative activities like brand marketing and events that attribution models would incorrectly recommend cutting.

Strategic hookWhen building a business case for marketing investments in AI-visibility channels, use triangulation and incrementality testing instead of relying on attribution alone to avoid underinvesting in activities that drive demand through unmeasurable pathways.

The article explores money-back guarantees as an alternative to free trials for subscription apps, arguing they can be strategically powerful when deployed correctly. Strategic hookUnderstanding how different activation mechanisms (free trial vs. paid upfront with guarantee) attract different customer segments and affect long-term retention helps teams design go-to-market strategies that align pricing friction with acquisition channel quality and business margin realities.
