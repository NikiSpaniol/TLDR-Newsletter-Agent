THE PROMPT-TO-PURCHASE PIPELINE: WHAT MILLIONS OF SEARCH EVENTS TOLD US ABOUT HOW AI INFLUENCES BUYER BEHAVIOR (8 MINUTE READ)

hook

Scrunch analyzed millions of anonymized search events from February to May 2026, linking AI platform conversations (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) to subsequent web activity like Google searches, website visits, and retailer product views.

Scrunch analyzed millions of anonymized search events from February to May 2026, linking AI platform conversations (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) to subsequent web activity like Google searches, website visits, and retailer product views. When AI platforms actively recommended a brand to new customers, those users became 182% more likely to search the brand on Google, 117% more likely to visit the brand's website, and 185% more likely to view products on retailer pages within a week. ChatGPT drove significantly larger funnel movement than Gemini, though the difference partly reflected ChatGPT users being more purchase-ready overall. Recommendations outperformed mere mentions by roughly double at each funnel stage, and brands named first in AI answers or framed as "best" or "top" saw substantially higher downstream search likelihood (389% and 425% lift respectively). The research reveals that traditional attribution dashboards miss AI's contribution to early-funnel intent activity, creating a measurement gap that could lead brands to underinvest in AI search optimization.

Strategic hookBrands need to understand how AI platforms are creating awareness and preference among new customer segments to properly evaluate which channels deserve marketing investment and how customer discovery journeys are evolving.

hook

Reddit and WPP Media research shows that modern consumers face decision paralysis due to information overload and skepticism, pushing them to seek human validation before committing to purchases. The study identifies a critical "validation layer" in the consideration phase where 1 in 3 redditors conduct pre-purchase research, with 77% visiting multiple communities to validate decisions and 63% reporting greater confidence in Reddit-based decisions versus other platforms. Strategic hookThis research directly supports customer-journey mapping by identifying the critical validation/consideration phase where purchase decisions are actively shaped, enabling brands to understand where and how to intervene with relevant, helpful content to build buyer confidence.

THE CHOICES THAT DETERMINE WHETHER YOUR MMM (MARKETING MIX MODEL) ACTUALLY WORKS (10 MINUTE READ)

hook

Marketing Mix Models can drive sound budget decisions, but the modeling technique matters far less than the upstream design choices.

Marketing Mix Models can drive sound budget decisions, but the modeling technique matters far less than the upstream design choices. Co-authors Raunak Kumar and team identify three prerequisites before building an MMM: at least 18 months of consistent weekly-level spend and outcome data, meaningful week-to-week budget variation (not flat budgets), and paid media driving 20–30% or more of top-of-funnel activity. They then walk through six critical decisions that reshape results: aligning on stakeholder definition of success before picking a metric (signups, pipeline, or revenue each tell a different story); choosing the right outcome variable by understanding tradeoffs rather than defaulting to convenience; segmenting self-serve and sales-led motions separately rather than collapsing them into one response curve; distinguishing between adstock decay windows (how fast individual exposures lose influence) and decision horizons (how long sustained campaigns take to compound); presenting uncertainty ranges instead of point estimates in planning decks; and accepting that strategic questions like growth-versus-efficiency tradeoffs cannot be answered by the model alone. The authors emphasize that mature MMM practice means making decisions explicit, running quarterly refreshes, and pairing models with incrementality tests rather than treating either as sufficient alone.

Strategic hookAligning stakeholders on the specific definition of success (signups, pipeline, or revenue) before building an MMM informs the right customer outcome metric to track, ensuring marketing budget decisions serve the actual business strategy rather than creating conflicting interpretations across Growth, Sales, and Finance functions.

AMERICANS AND AI 2026: CHATBOTS, SMART DEVICES, AND VIEWS ON IMPACT (8 MINUTE READ)

hook

Pew Research Center conducted a survey of 5,119 U.S.

Pew Research Center conducted a survey of 5,119 U.S. adults in February 2026 to measure American adoption and attitudes toward artificial intelligence. The study found that chatbot usage has nearly doubled since 2024, with 49% of adults now using tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, or Copilot—up from 33% in 2024—and about 24% using them daily. ChatGPT dominates the market at 44% adoption, while other platforms trail significantly (Gemini at 24%, Copilot at 17%). The most common uses are information searching (42% of users) and work tasks (38% of employed users), though 10% use them for emotional support. Beyond chatbots, 35% of Americans have smart speakers and smaller shares own smart doorbells (18%), robot vacuums (13%), and smart thermostats (11%). Despite widespread adoption, Americans are deeply skeptical: 40% believe AI will negatively impact society over the next 20 years versus only 16% predicting positive effects, and 63% say AI is advancing too quickly. Notably, younger adults (under 30) are significantly more pessimistic about AI's impact than older groups despite using these tools more frequently.

Strategic hookUnderstanding the divergence between high AI adoption rates and widespread negative sentiment toward AI's societal impact is critical for businesses developing customer-facing AI products, as it reveals a need to conduct deeper research into customer concerns and develop trust-building strategies that address privacy, job displacement, and speed-of-change anxieties.

DO AI ASSISTANTS ACTUALLY RENDER YOUR JAVASCRIPT WHEN GROUNDING? WE PUT IT TO THE TEST (8 MINUTE READ)

A client question sparked an investigation into whether AI assistants actually execute JavaScript when users ask them to summarize web pages.

A client question sparked an investigation into whether AI assistants actually execute JavaScript when users ask them to summarize web pages. The researchers tested twelve AI assistants by giving each a unique URL containing a decoy value in raw HTML and a real value hidden behind executed JavaScript, then checked server logs to see which assistants ran the code. The results showed a stark geographic split: all six major US assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Meta AI, Microsoft Copilot) read only raw HTML and reported the decoy, while Chinese assistants (DeepSeek, ERNIE, Qwen, Kimi) and Mistral rendered JavaScript and reported the real value. The research revealed that assistants' self-descriptions of their own behavior often contradict server logs—notably Perplexity claimed it couldn't access a page it had actually fetched—underscoring that measurement, not claims, reveals truth. For content strategists, the takeaway is direct: if JavaScript-rendered content is important for AI discovery, it will be invisible to the dominant US assistants unless pre-rendered or server-side rendered into the HTML.

ONE METRIC FOR TOTAL MARKETING SPEND (2 MINUTE READ)

hook

The article explains Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER), a metric that divides total revenue by total marketing spend to assess overall marketing performance across all channels and campaigns.

The article explains Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER), a metric that divides total revenue by total marketing spend to assess overall marketing performance across all channels and campaigns. Unlike channel-specific metrics like ROAS, MER provides a blended view of total business performance—for example, a company generating $500,000 in revenue on $100,000 in marketing spend has a MER of 5, meaning $5 revenue per $1 spent. The metric works best when calculated consistently by including all marketing costs: advertising, agency fees, affiliate commissions, influencer expenses, software subscriptions, creative production, and labor. MER sidesteps attribution debates by ignoring which specific touchpoint drove each sale and instead helps marketers establish spending guardrails based on profitability thresholds. The appropriate MER varies by business model—a retailer with 70% gross margin can tolerate lower efficiency than one with 25%. MER complements rather than replaces channel-specific analysis and helps shift focus from vanity metrics like clicks to actual revenue and profit.

Strategic hookMarketing leaders can use MER as a business case tool to determine whether total marketing investment achieves acceptable return relative to company margins and profitability targets, enabling data-driven budget allocation decisions across the entire marketing mix.
