DOUBLE-DIGIT ECOMMERCE GROWTH RETURNS (2 MINUTE READ)

hook

U.S.

U.S. ecommerce sales grew 12.2% year-over-year in Q2 2026 and 10.1% in Q1, marking two consecutive quarters of double-digit growth that resembles pre-pandemic patterns, with first-half 2026 totaling $668.1 billion in seasonally adjusted sales. The growth accelerated from single-digit rates in 2025 (5.0% to 5.9% YoY) and outpaced overall retail growth of 6.7%, pushing ecommerce's share of total U.S. retail to 17.1% from 16.3% year-over-year. Jon Reeves at VaynerMedia argues that marketing has fallen into an "efficiency trap" where brands optimize for short-term metrics like impressions and reach while disconnecting from actual business growth. Since 2019, marketing efficiency has increased 63% through programmatic and AI buying, yet brand growth, market share, and long-term profit have fallen 26%. The core problem is that "potential reach" differs drastically from actual attention—studies show only 50% of pixels need to be visible for one to two seconds to count as an impression, 85% of digital ads fail to meet the 2.5-second threshold for brand memory, and billions in ad spend reaches clickbait sites. The thesis is simple: brands must choose between paying to be "served" (delivered) or working to be "seen" (actually noticed and remembered).

COPYWRITING FRIDAY: DUMB WAYS TO DIE (4 MINUTE READ)

hook

In 2012, Metro Trains Melbourne and McCann created "Dumb Ways to Die," a music video campaign that reached over 340 million views and won five Grand Prix awards at Cannes Lions by transforming an unwanted safety message into engaging entertainment. Rather than making the warning clearer, the creative team changed the medium entirely—from public service announcement to music video and later a mobile game that hit number one in 22 countries—making the actual safety message ride in on the back of content people genuinely wanted to consume. Strategic hookThis work directly applies to customer-journey mapping by demonstrating how to restructure the sequence of messaging and friction points—strategically delaying necessary but unwelcome information until after you have established audience engagement and receptivity, rather than leading with barriers.

However, even well-performing listicles face declining click opportunity: 92% of listicle SERPs contain Reddit or YouTube, and 83.7–93.4% of B2B listicle queries display AI Overviews, fragmenting attention away from organic results.

Strategic hookB2B companies prioritizing AI search optimization should analyze whether their self-serving listicles receive third-party credibility signals (G2 rankings, customer reviews) before investing in the tactic, given evidence that Google favors independent publisher lists and that most listicle queries now compete heavily with Reddit, YouTube, and AI-generated answers for user attention.
